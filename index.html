About the game

Snugbox is a calm block puzzle. Drag pieces into an 8 × 8 box, and every full row or column pops. There is no timer. Three twists of its own: the Pocket keeps a piece for later, Sparks clear a whole row and column, and the Streak multiplies your score.

Common questions

My progress disappeared. Your coins, collection, Box Level and best scores are stored on your iPhone. Deleting the app, or its data, deletes them too.

I bought coins and they have not arrived. Close the game completely and open it again: a purchase that was interrupted is delivered the next time the game starts. If they are still missing, email us with the date of the purchase and we will sort it out.

I bought Remove Ads and still see ads. Remove Ads stops the ads between runs. Rewarded videos, the ones you choose to watch for a bonus, stay, because they only ever play when you tap for one. If ads between runs come back, tap Restore Purchases in the Store or in Settings.

I have a new iPhone. Tap Restore Purchases to bring back Remove Ads. Coins and progress stay on the old device; they are not synced.

There is no sound. Check the silent switch on the side of your iPhone, then the sound setting in the game's Settings.

Refunds

Purchases go through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple rather than by us. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. Tell us as well if something went wrong. It usually means there is a bug worth fixing.

Privacy

Your game progress stays on your iPhone. The game shows advertising. When you first open the game you are asked whether to allow tracking; you can change that at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone. In the EEA and the UK, the game's Settings → Privacy options lets you change your ad consent.
