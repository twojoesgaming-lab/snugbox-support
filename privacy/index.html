Privacy Policy

Snugbox · Two Joes Gaming
Last updated 22 November 2026 (tracking prompt timing)

The short version

We do not run any servers and there are no accounts, so we hold nothing about you ourselves. Your game progress lives on your iPhone. The game shows advertising, and the advertising is where any data collection happens.

What stays on your iPhone

Your coins, boosters, collection, Box Level, missions, Daily Box medals, best scores, settings and a run in progress are stored in the app's own storage on your iPhone. They are not sent anywhere, and we cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and asks for consent where the law requires it through Google's User Messaging Platform. To do that, Google's software may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier, used for tracking only if you allow it;
  • advertising data, such as which ads were shown and which you tapped;
  • product interaction, such as when the app was opened and how ads were reached;
  • an approximate location derived from your IP address, never your precise location (the game never asks for location permission);
  • performance, crash and other diagnostic data.

It is used to show and measure ads and, if you allow tracking, to personalise them. You are asked whether to allow tracking when you first open the game. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, you still see ads and the game plays exactly the same. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, you are asked for consent first, and the game's Settings → Privacy options lets you change it later.

Google's own policy explains what they do with this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Coin packs and Remove Ads are sold by Apple, not by us. Apple handles the payment and tells the app only whether a purchase succeeded. We never see your card details, your Apple Account, your name or your address. The purchase record stays on your iPhone.

Children

Snugbox is a general-audience game and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, email us and we will help.

Your choices

  • Turn off ad tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • In the EEA and the UK, change your ad consent in the game's Settings → Privacy options.
  • Buy Remove Ads to stop the ads between runs.
  • Delete the app to remove everything it has stored.
  • Email us with any question about your data and we will answer it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
